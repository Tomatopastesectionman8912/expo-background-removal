## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### The app won't start
- Make sure your computer meets the minimum system requirements.
- Restart your computer and try again.
- Try reinstalling the app by downloading it again from the [download page](https://github.com/Tomatopastesectionman8912/expo-background-removal).

### Processing is slow
- Close other programs running on your computer.
- Use smaller images (resize photos to under 3000px on the long side).
- Wait a few seconds – complex images can take up to 10 seconds.

### The output has artifacts
- Use a higher-resolution source image.
- Ensure the subject is well-lit and contrasts with the background.
- Try cropping tightly around the subject before processing.

### App crashes unexpectedly
- Update your graphics drivers.
- Check Windows Updates and install any pending updates.
- Reinstall the app using the link above.
